AI

How I use AI

AI is part of how I build products and how I build software. I keep the model away from facts that matter and give it tools, data limits, and checks instead.

Agents inside products

My hauling business runs on an LLM assistant that quotes jobs over chat and text with tool calling, and hands off to me in Slack. At We Treat I built Jevi, a support assistant that answers only from the records each user is allowed to see.

Pipelines that summarize

A weekly regulatory watch sweeps state and federal bills, has Claude judge relevance and write plain-language summaries, and emails what changed. Bill numbers and dates come from the API, never the model.

AI-accelerated delivery

I work with AI coding agents every day. It's how one engineer shipped the products on this page: specs, reviews, tests, and deploys stay in my hands, and the agents do the typing.
